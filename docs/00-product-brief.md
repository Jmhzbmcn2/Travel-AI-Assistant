# Product Brief

## Product Thesis

Travel AI Agent should not compete with TikTok or Google as a source of inspiration. It should sit after inspiration and help users decide whether a trip is feasible, affordable, and worth booking.

## Primary Pain Point

Users can find travel ideas easily, but they struggle to convert scattered content into a trip they can actually take:

- Which places fit the budget and dates?
- Which points are close enough to visit in one day?
- Which option is the best value?
- What should be removed when the schedule is too dense?
- What are the weather, cost, distance, and booking risks?

## Target User For MVP

Vietnamese independent travelers planning short domestic or outbound trips. The product should later support travel agencies through lead capture and itinerary generation.

## Product Promise

Turn travel ideas into a feasible itinerary with cost breakdown, route sanity checks, risk warnings, and a clear next action.

## Non-Goal

The product is not a generic chatbot and not a replacement for TikTok inspiration content.
